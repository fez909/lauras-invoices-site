A personal desktop app

Less admin. More time for your practice.

Laura’s Invoices helps organise private-work sessions, create one invoice per session and record expenses, with records saved on your own laptop.

Sessions, invoices and expenses

Set rates for each provider, record full-hour or shorter appointments, and generate PDF invoices. Room costs are recorded separately as internal expenses and do not reduce the invoice amount.

Connect your Google Calendar

The optional Google connection reads your calendar list so you can choose your private-work calendar. It reads appointments to create and update planned sessions in the app. You review unfamiliar appointments and confirm completed sessions before invoicing.

The app requests read-only access. It does not change Google Calendar events, invite attendees or send emails. Automatic sync runs while the app is open and the laptop is awake.

Your laptop holds your records

The app is designed for macOS and Windows. It has no hosted database or developer-operated sync server. This website contains information only; you cannot upload client records here.

Read the privacy policy for details of Google access, local storage and backups, and the terms of use before using the app.

Availability and support

This is a private personal project, supplied directly to its intended users. Google Calendar integration is currently being tested.
