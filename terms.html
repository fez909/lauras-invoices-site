A tool for your administration.

Laura’s Invoices is a personal desktop project maintained by Michael Ferry and supplied directly to its intended users. These terms describe its intended use.

Check your records

You are responsible for reviewing session matches, rates, expenses, invoice details and payment information before relying on or sharing them. Calendar imports create planned sessions; confirm that work has been completed before issuing an invoice.

The app helps organise records. It does not provide clinical, accounting, tax or legal advice, and its expense records do not determine whether an expense is deductible.

Use only authorised information

Connect only Google accounts and calendars you are authorised to use. Keep identifying details to the minimum needed and follow the rules that apply to your professional work.

Look after local data

Protect your laptop and keep suitable backups. The app does not provide a hosted backup or recovery service. Exported files and backups need to be handled securely.

Availability

This is a developing personal project. Features may change, and Google integration depends on Google’s services, permissions and policies. Check important records after updates or connection problems. No paid service or support subscription is offered through this website.

Privacy and contact

The privacy policy explains data handling. For help or to report a problem, contact Michael Ferry at michaelaferry@gmail.com. Do not include client or clinical information in a bug report.
