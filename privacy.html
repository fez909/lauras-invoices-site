Privacy policy · 5 October 2026

Your information, explained.

This policy covers Laura’s Invoices, a local desktop app maintained by Michael Ferry, and this public information website.

Information you enter

The app stores practitioner and payment details, provider details and rates, client references, session dates and locations, invoices, payment status and expenses. These records are stored in a database on the computer running the app. Use client references rather than unnecessary identifying or clinical details.

Google Calendar access

Connecting Google is optional. Google handles sign-in in your browser; the app does not receive your Google password. The app requests permission to read your calendar list and events. Google’s event permission applies to all calendars you can access; the app uses your selected calendar for session synchronisation.

The app uses calendar names and time zones, event identifiers, titles, start and end times, locations, cancellation status and whether a remote-meeting link is present. It uses these to match appointments to client references, create planned sessions, update appointments and prevent duplicates. Google may return additional event fields, such as descriptions and attendees; the app does not retain those fields.

Completed sessions and issued invoices are protected from automatic calendar changes. Appointments needing a decision are shown for review. Calendar access is read-only: the app does not create, edit or delete Google events.

Storage and security

Google access and refresh tokens are stored separately using the operating system’s secure-storage encryption. They are not included in app backups. The local session and invoice database, exported JSON backups and PDF invoices are not encrypted by the app. Protect the computer and any backup locations appropriately.

Sharing and use

The app communicates directly with Google for authorisation and calendar sync. It does not upload your records to the maintainer, operate a central account database, use advertising or analytics trackers, sell personal information, or use Google data to train AI models. Files you export are shared only when you choose to share them using another application or service.

Laura’s Invoices’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and disconnecting

Records remain on your computer until you remove them. Disconnecting Google removes the local sign-in tokens and attempts to revoke access at Google; it keeps existing sessions and invoices. You can also revoke access through your Google Account’s third-party connections settings. An offline disconnect may require this extra step.

Uninstalling the app may leave its data folder behind. To remove all local records, remove that folder and any exported PDFs or backups you no longer need. Contact the maintainer for help locating them. Keep any records you are required to retain.

This website

This site uses GitHub Pages. It has no forms, analytics scripts or advertising cookies. GitHub may process visitor connection information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the site. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact Michael Ferry at michaelaferry@gmail.com. Do not send client records or clinical information by email. The maintainer cannot access or delete records held only on your laptop.

Changes to data handling will be reflected on this page, with an updated date.
